Privacy Policy

Sheehy & Sheehy, Esqs. — Social Media & Workflow Automation Tools

Last updated: July 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Sheehy & Sheehy, Esqs. ("we," "us," or "our"), located in Toms River, New Jersey, handles information in connection with our internal workflow automation and social media publishing tools, including applications that connect to Facebook and other Meta platforms (the "App").

Who We Are

The App is an internal business tool operated solely by and for Sheehy & Sheehy. It is used to manage and publish content to our own social media pages and to automate our own business workflows. It is not offered to the general public as a product or service.

Information We Collect

The App accesses only the data necessary to perform its functions, which may include:

  • Page and account information for social media pages we own or administer (page name, page ID, access tokens).
  • Content we publish through the App (posts, images, captions, and scheduling information).
  • Engagement metrics made available by the platform (such as likes, comments, and reach) for pages we administer.

The App does not collect, request, or store personal information from members of the public, followers, or visitors to our pages. We do not sell personal information to anyone.

How We Use Information

Information accessed by the App is used only to:

  • Publish and schedule content to social media pages we own;
  • Review the performance of our own published content;
  • Operate and troubleshoot our internal automation workflows.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade any information accessed by the App. Information is not shared with third parties, except for the service providers that host our own infrastructure, and only as necessary to operate the App.

Data Storage and Security

Data accessed by the App is stored on secure, access-controlled systems maintained by our firm. Access tokens and credentials are stored encrypted and are accessible only to authorized personnel. We retain data only as long as necessary for the purposes described above.

Data Deletion

If you believe the App has stored data relating to you and you would like it deleted, contact us at the email address below and we will delete it promptly. To revoke the App's access to your Facebook data (if you have interacted with it), you may also remove the App via your Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites.

Children's Privacy

The App is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
